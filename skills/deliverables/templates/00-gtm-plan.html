01Executive summary

€900kARR today, 14 plants
8 plantsnew in 12 months
32–40qualified opportunities needed
≤ €4kper month, no marketing hire

Fluxen wants 8 new mid-size plants in 12 months while breaking the founder's monopoly on origination. The bet: audit-ready energy intelligence for plants without an energy manager, walking away from the sustainability conversation the giants own and competing instead against spreadsheets and audit panic. At ~25% proposal close on a 2–4 month cycle, that takes 32–40 qualified opportunities against ~20 last year, generated by engines the current team can run: systematized referrals, audit-deadline ABM on ~120 accounts, compliance content that compounds into year 2, and an integrator pilot. The first 90 days commit six initiatives, gated by the founder's capacity of four live opportunities.

~20 opportunities a year, ~60% from a saturated founder network. No instrumented CRM.

Constraints

≤ €4k a month, no marketing hire, founder carries ~4 opportunities at once.

08Execution readiness

This plan decides positioning and demand. It does not build the machinery that converts and operates them.

Workstream 1

Conversion design

Qualification criteria, routing and founder-capacity rules, pipeline stages, the sales playbook. Scheduled for Q3.

If skippedThe engines feed a bottleneck, and the plan gets blamed for a conversion problem.
Workstream 2

Deployment

Content production cadence, ABM operations, partner-program mechanics.

If skippedEngines start, then stall on the first busy month.
Workstream 3

Measurement

Source and stage instrumentation (roadmap #1 is the prerequisite), reporting cadence, engine-level review.

If skippedBy month three, budget follows opinions.

Executing these workstreams takes either internal revenue-operations capacity or a specialized partner. The roadmap assumes founder time, one CS lead and freelance content; that gap is the thing to audit internally.
