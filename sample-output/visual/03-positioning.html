04Messages by funnel stage

Awareness and decision must not sound alike

Awareness · present the category

"Mid-size plants fail energy audits for the same three reasons. None of them needs an energy manager to fix."

Consideration · push to the conversation

"We'll show you, on your own meter data, what an auditor would flag, in a 30-minute session."

Decision · kill objections, create urgency

"Your next audit is dated. Live in days, first report in week one, or you walk away."

  • Leading with price against integratorsThe fight is value against doing nothing, not cost against dashboards.
