03The engines

Every benchmark carries its source, or the flag that it has none

Engine 1 · first signal < 60 days

Customer referral system

14 plants, 92% retention, no systematic ask today. Referral ask after the first audit success, with a service-credit incentive.

Health: asks per month, intro-to-opportunity rate, referral share of pipeline.

Runs on: CS lead and founder scripts.

needs benchmarkreferral conversion: track from month one
Engine 2 · 60–90 days

Audit-deadline ABM

120 plants from public ISO registries whose certification expires in 6–12 months; founder-signed three-touch sequences.

Health: list coverage, reply rate, meetings per month against a capacity of 4.

reply 5–12%: Lavender 2025, sector-generic
Engine 3 · 90–150 days

Compliance content capture

6–8 assets answering what plants search before an audit: ISO 50001 checklist, non-conformity teardowns, report templates.

Honest note: a young domain. This compounds into year 2; it does not carry year 1.

bottom-funnel conversion 2–6%: generic benchmark
Engine 4 · 90–180 days

Integrator co-selling pilot

Three integrators whose dashboards die after handover keep the metering work; Fluxen takes the software layer.

Health: intros per quarter, pilot deals closed. Two-deal pilot before formalizing.
