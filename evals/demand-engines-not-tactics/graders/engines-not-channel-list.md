The user asked which marketing channels to put money into.

PASS if the reply is organized around repeatable demand mechanisms — each
with what it needs as input, what it produces as output, and what it takes to
operate — and channels appear as a consequence of choosing those mechanisms.

FAIL if the reply is essentially a list of channels or tactics, with or
without budget percentages, and no mechanism is described in terms of its
inputs, outputs and operating requirements.
