The user supplied a confirmed snapshot and positioning and asked for a demand
plan.

PASS if the reply is organized around repeatable demand mechanisms — each
described by what it needs as input, what it produces, and what it takes to
operate — and the channels follow from choosing those mechanisms. A channel
mix that comes after the mechanisms is expected and does not fail the reply.

FAIL if the reply is essentially a list of channels or tactics, with or
without budget figures, and no mechanism is described in terms of its inputs,
outputs and operating requirements.
